Lichess permits downloading, modifying, and redistributing its database exports.
The pack includes original puzzle IDs, ratings, theme tags, source game URLs,
FEN positions and complete UCI solution lines. No runtime connection is needed.
Ratings describe puzzle difficulty on Lichess, not a Knightline player rating.
The first move of each stored line is the opponent's setup move; the second is
the first player move. All packaged lines are replayed through native chess
legality tests. The six original Knightline warm-ups remain separately defined.

Import: tools/import-puzzles.mjs. 50 positions in each range 800-1199,
1200-1599, 1600-1999, 2000-2399 and 2400-3000. At least two player moves per
new puzzle; popularity >=85, >=1000 plays, rating deviation <=90. Motif quotas
prevent one tactical pattern dominating a difficulty band.
